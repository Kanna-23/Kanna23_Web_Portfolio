Royal Pop — Swatch × Audemars Piguet | Rendu 3D produit

As part of a personal project, I created a series of 3D renders of the Royal Pop, available in 8 distinct colorways.

Each colorway was given its own independent visual treatment, in order to best capture the identity specific to each shade. This precision-driven work reflects my approach to product rendering: rigor, realism, and attention to detail.
