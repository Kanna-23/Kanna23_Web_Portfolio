Project information

CotCotBoom | Unity game

CotCotBOOM is a dynamic game in which players compete by team around a fleeing and explosive chicken. If the chicken explodes in a team’s camp, it gives a point to its opponents, so try to grab the animal in order to send it to the opposing cages or don’t let it explode in your camp!

On this project, I was able to realize the environment assets.
