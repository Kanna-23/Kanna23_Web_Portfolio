Wine Bottles

Blender, Substance Painter 2024

Project information

Art de Vivre Wines Bottles | 3D Render Product

I was introduced to Art de Vivre wines and their uniquely designed bottles, inspired by ceramic amphorae. Captivated by their unusual aesthetics and distinctive texture, I wanted to translate all their elegance through my work.

For this project, I paid particular attention to the materials and lighting, in order to highlight the ceramic texture and the subtle interplay of light on the bottle's surface. My goal was to create a sleek and refined rendering, playing on the details to emphasize the authentic and premium aspect of the object.
