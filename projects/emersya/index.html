Emersya | 3D Render Product for web

Following my studies, I held the position of 3D modeler for two years at the startup Emersya. There, I designed and optimized 3D models intended for real-time product configurators for the web. My role included the preparation and optimization of 3D scans, as well as the creation of lightweight and efficient models tailored to the technical constraints of the web.

I worked with various specialized tools such as Blender, Substance Painter, RealityCapture, and InstaLOD, which allowed me to master the entire 3D production pipeline. At Emersya, I contributed to projects developed for different brands, participating in the creation of 3D models used in their interactive presentations.
