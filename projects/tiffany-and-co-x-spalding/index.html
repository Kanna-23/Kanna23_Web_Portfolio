Project information

Tiffany and Co X Spalding | 3D Render Product

The objective of the project is to recreate in 3D the unique collaboration between Tiffany & Co and Spalding. Additionally, I created a pair of Air Jordan Retro 3 inspired by the collaboration.

The main challenge was to capture the texture of the ball and to convey the famous Tiffany blue with lighting that enhances every detail. An unexpected fusion between two worlds that I wanted to express.
